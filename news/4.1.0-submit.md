# A tool for sending in a pack

A few people have said they'd like to build soundpacks for the app. Until now that meant reading a guide that was far too long and still not much help — so I've built the tool instead. It puts together a **sample pack** for the Sample Browser and, if you want one, a **preset** to go with it.

The three words the app uses:

- **Sample pack** — a collection of samples, downloaded from **Browse Samples** or sent to your SD card.
- **Project** — what you work on in **Studio**: tapes with samples in their slots, notes and a `config.txt`, synced to your card, kept on your own computer.
- **Preset** — a project built from sample packs, up to 36 files in the 6×6 grid. Write it straight to a card from the **Presets** door, or import it into Studio and tweak it.

The tool asks how your pack should appear in the app — text, categories, sample titles, links, image — and hands you a ZIP at the end. Send that to me (jonwtr) over whatever cloud service you like.

## What the six steps ask

Drop a folder in and its subfolder names become the categories people filter by. Titles are editable in place, files over 42 seconds are flagged, and there's an optional audio editor (normalize, trim, EQ, …).

![Step 2 — the audio](/img/docs/submit-step2-audio.webp)

A preset is optional and comes second: the same 6×6 grid Studio uses, with its tape notes, built from your own sounds or mixed with packs already in the app. Slots drag, and the tool works out which packs the layout depends on by itself.

![Step 5 — the preset grid](/img/docs/submit-step5-preset.webp)

Review says what's still missing before anything is sent, then builds the archive — and an `SK/` folder for your own card from the same draft.

![Step 6 — review and send](/img/docs/submit-step6-review.webp)

## Two things worth knowing

**Nothing is lost.** The draft saves itself as you go, and the archive reopens: drop the ZIP back onto step 2, on any machine, and the whole form returns — files, details, licence, preset and all.

**You may already have a submission.** A pool assembled in Browse and a project open in Studio are each one in everything but name; look for the *send to preset submission* button and either goes straight across.

Questions are welcome on Discord (@jonwtr), and the written guide sits behind the Guide button in the tool itself. And take 235 of the tutorial video might just be the one I upload, any day now…

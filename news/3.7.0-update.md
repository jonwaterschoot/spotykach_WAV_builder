# Portable Export & Preset Sharing (v3.7.0)

This update brings powerful new ways to share your work and prepare your SD card for the Spotykach hardware.

### What's new:
- **Portable Export Module**: A dedicated "Portable SK Folder" export that automatically bundles hardware-compatible structures and instructions.
- **Project Preset Sharing**: You can now export "Settings-Only" JSON presets. This makes sharing your project configurations incredibly fast, especially when using cloud-hosted samples.
- **Refactored Export UI**: The Export Modal is now modular and tabbed, making it easier to choose between hardware prep, preset sharing, or full backups.

### Improvements:
- **Navigation**: Enhanced the "Project" dropdown and moved export actions there for a cleaner header.
- **Hainbach Presets**: Updated the official Hainbach pack links for better hardware compatibility.

# Documentation Hub & Tab Reorganization (v3.7.3)

This release focuses on centralizing our documentation structure, integrating screenshots & video guides into the app, and linking together user, artist, and developer guides.

### What's new:
- **Sample & Preset Guide Tab**: Replaced the Help modal tab with a streamlined "Sample & Preset Guide" covering:
  - **Concepts Explainer**: Comparison matrix of Sample Packs vs. Project Presets.
  - **Media Walkthroughs**: Vertical screenshot & video walkthrough cards for both the Sample Browser and the Preset Manager.
  - **Copy-to-Clipboard Checklist**: Monospace template box equipped with a copy icon and success micro-animations for easy metadata submission.
- **Unified Documentation Links**: Added an "App Maintainer & Developer Resources" section in the Help modal, linking to the preset upload guide, audio scripts metadata, and schema files on GitHub.

### Reorganization:
- **Unified Directories**: Renamed and moved `docs/submittingpresets/` to the cleaner `/docs/presets-samples/` layout, deleting duplicate files.
- **Refactored Guides**:
  - Keeps `/docs/presets-samples/README.md` strictly user/artist-focused.
  - Moved app maintainer steps to `/public/presets/preset_upload_guide.md` alongside local presets setup guides.

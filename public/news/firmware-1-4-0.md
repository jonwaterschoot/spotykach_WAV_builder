# Firmware 1.4.0: USB transfer is on

Spotykach firmware **1.4.0** is out of beta: get it from [Synthux](https://www.synthux.academy/firmware/spotykach/spotykach/v1.4.0).

With it, WAV Builder reaches the SD card while it stays in the Spotykach, through the rear USB port, and USB transfer is now on by default. The sync window has its SD | USB switch, and a preset can go onto the card over USB. If you only use a card reader, turn it off in **Settings ▸ System**.

Still worth knowing: it needs Chrome or Edge on a computer and a good data cable straight into a USB-A port. The card reader stays faster for a full card: over USB a full slot takes about 35 seconds.

Cue points in the config editor lose their Beta label too. Snap and Free work from firmware 1.3.0 on.

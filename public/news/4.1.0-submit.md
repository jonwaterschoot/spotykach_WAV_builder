# A tool for sending in a pack

I have finally completed a submission tool. I already had a good behind the scenes way to handle Sample Packs, but not a convenient way to submit it.

Because a few people have said they wanted to make soundpacks to integrate in the app, I had written a guide; but this guide was way too long, and still not that helpful.

So now we have a submission tool that allows to easily build two things: **Sample packs** for the Sample Browser and a way to build **presets**.

Look at the guide if you're confused by what the difference is between Sample packs, Presets, projects.

In summary:

- A **sample pack** = a collection of samples you can manually download or add to the SD card tools
  - In the main hub pick: **Browse Samples**
  - In the main hub pick: **Studio**

- A **project** = used in Studio:
  - a set of tapes with samples assigned to the slots
  - can contain notes and a dedicated config.txt
  - has a sync option to read and build your SD card
  - stored on your own computer
  - create and switch between multiple projects

- A **preset** = a project that has been made from the sample packs
  - can contain up to 36 files into the 6×6 slots
  - lives in the app on server, needs to be imported, downloaded or written to SD
  - from the hub door "Presets": can be written straight to an SD card
  - can be imported into the Studio for further tweaking
  - could be used as a way to trade projects between users

By using the tool you can customize how your pack will be presented in the app. Things you review are: text, optional categories, with or without a preset, sample titles (can be derived from folder and filenames), links, image, etc.

At the end you download a ZIP, and then you send it to me (jonwtr) via your own cloud service for further review.

## What the six steps ask

Drop a folder in and its subfolder names become the categories people filter by. Titles are editable in place, files over 42 seconds are flagged, there's an optional audio editor available (normalization, trimming, eq, ...).

![Step 2 — the audio](/img/docs/submit-step2-audio.webp)

A preset is optional and comes second: the same 6×6 grid Studio uses, with its tape notes, built from your own sounds or mixed with packs already in the app. Slots drag, and the tool works out which packs the layout depends on by itself.

![Step 5 — the preset grid](/img/docs/submit-step5-preset.webp)

Review says what is still missing before anything is sent, then builds the archive. It will also build an `SK/` folder for your own card from the same draft.

![Step 6 — review and send](/img/docs/submit-step6-review.webp)

## Two things worth knowing

Your draft is saved in your browser session as you go, and the archive reopens. Though safer is to export it and drop it back onto step 2 (on any machine) and the whole form comes back (files, details, licence, preset, ...)

You may already have a submission:

- A pool assembled in Browse and a project open in Studio are both one in everything but name, and either can be sent straight across. (look for the "send to preset submission" button)
